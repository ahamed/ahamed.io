## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Heliopause stop and route end

After Eris, the route SHALL pass the heliopause stop, the last stop. Its spacing SHALL follow the same log-compressed rule as every other stop, from a real gap of about 25 AU. The stop SHALL have no visible body of its own; an invisible anchor beside the path SHALL open its dialog when the camera is parked there, like any other stop. After the stop, the route SHALL continue through interstellar space into the finale (the Pale Blue Dot look-back and the contact card) and end there.

#### Scenario: Twenty-seven stops in order

- **WHEN** the visitor travels from progress 0 to 1
- **THEN** the heliopause stop is passed after Eris and is the last stop, and the route then ends in the finale

#### Scenario: Heliopause dialog opens when parked

- **WHEN** the camera rests at the heliopause stop's close point
- **THEN** the heliopause dialog opens, and it does not cover Voyager
